Plan Pace privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026.

Plan Pace is an independent app. It is not made by Anthropic and it is not affiliated with Anthropic. This policy describes what the app does with information on your phone.

What stays on your phone

After you sign in, the app stores:

  • The login tokens that let the app read plan usage. These are kept in the phone’s secure storage (Android Keystore or iOS Keychain).
  • Usage percentages, reset times, and a short list of recent checks, so the screen and the Android home screen widget can show them.
  • The times of reset notifications the app has scheduled.

The app does not read your chats, files, contacts, photos, or location. It does not include advertising or analytics. The person who published the app does not receive your login or your usage numbers.

What is sent off the phone

To sign in and to refresh the login, the app contacts Claude’s login service (platform.claude.com and, if needed, console.anthropic.com). The sign-in page itself opens on claude.com.

To read usage, the app sends the login token to https://api.anthropic.com/api/oauth/usage. Anthropic returns the usage percentages and reset times. That is the same kind of request Claude’s own tools make. It is not a documented public API, and Anthropic can change or block it.

Those requests use HTTPS. The app does not send the usage numbers to anyone else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification for each reset time. That notification is created on the phone. It is not a remote push from a server.

Deleting your information

Sign out inside Plan Pace deletes the login tokens and the saved usage from this phone. Uninstalling the app deletes what remains on the device. Plan Pace does not have its own account.

Your Claude account belongs to Anthropic. To delete that account, use the account settings on claude.ai. Signing out of Plan Pace does not delete the Claude account.

Children

Plan Pace is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change.
