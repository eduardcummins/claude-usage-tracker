Cluse privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

Cluse is an independent app. It is not made by Anthropic and it is not affiliated with Anthropic. This policy describes what the app does with information on your phone.

What stays on your phone

After you save a topic, the app stores:

  • The ntfy topic you pasted, and the server address (normally ntfy.sh). The topic is the key that lets this phone read the usage report.
  • Usage percentages, reset times, and a short list of recent checks, so the screen and the Android home screen widget can show them.
  • The times of reset notifications the app has scheduled.

The app does not read your chats, files, contacts, photos, or location. It does not include advertising or analytics. The person who published the app does not receive your topic or your usage numbers.

What is sent off the phone

The phone does not sign in to Claude. A helper on your computer reads the plan with the Claude Code login already on that computer.

If you scan or paste a pairing code, the computer encrypts the usage report before sending it. The encryption key is in that code. It is not sent to the relay. The relay stores only the encrypted report, keeps the latest one for about 12 hours, and then deletes it. The person who runs the relay cannot read the percentages.

A topic that starts with cu- still works. For that older path, the app requests the latest cached report from ntfy.sh (or another ntfy server, if you pasted a URL for one). That request uses HTTPS. Anyone who knows the topic can read the same percentages. The app does not send the usage numbers to the person who published Cluse.

If you tap Scan QR code, the phone uses the camera to read that code. The picture is not saved and it is not uploaded. You can paste the topic instead and never grant the camera.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules a local notification for each reset time. That notification is created on the phone. It is not a remote push from a server.

Deleting your information

Remove topic inside Cluse deletes the saved topic and the saved usage from this phone. Uninstalling the app deletes what remains on the device. Cluse does not have its own account.

Your Claude account belongs to Anthropic. To delete that account, use the account settings on claude.ai. Removing the topic from Cluse does not delete the Claude account.

Children

Cluse is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of this page will change.
